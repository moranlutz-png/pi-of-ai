Watch an AI learn to play Pong

Classic Pong: the green AI paddle on the left against a white opponent on the right — miss the ball anywhere down your side and they score. Nobody ever shows the AI the right move — it's only ever told its score and told to make it bigger. Each round it tries 28 slightly different tiny "brains", keeps the ones that scored higher, and mutates them (the same core idea behind DeepMind's game-players). Press Train and watch it go from losing every point to beating the opponent.

The AI's brain, live

inputs → decision up stay down

How it learns: this is reinforcement learning by evolution. A "brain" is a tiny neural net (5 inputs — where the ball is, where it's heading, where its own paddle is — through 8 hidden units to 3 choices: up / stay / down). It is never told the correct move. Each generation, all 28 brains play a full match against the opponent bot; the ones that finished with the best point difference survive and get mutated copies. Selection + mutation, repeated, is enough to discover a winning policy from the score alone. The opponent tracks the ball but moves a little slower than the AI, so it's beatable. It all runs in your browser — nothing downloaded, nothing sent anywhere.
