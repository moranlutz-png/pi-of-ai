Watch an AI learn to play Pong

Classic Pong: the green AI paddle on the left against a white opponent on the right — miss the ball anywhere down your side and they score. Nobody ever shows the AI the right move — it's only ever told its score and told to make it bigger. Each round it tries 28 slightly different tiny "brains", keeps the ones that scored higher, and mutates them (the same core idea behind DeepMind's game-players). Press Train and watch it go from flailing to reading the ball — moving to where it will land before it gets there — and beating the opponent. Then switch the opponent to Self-play to make it play itself and keep improving.

The AI's brain, live

How it learns: this is reinforcement learning by evolution. A "brain" is a tiny neural net (7 inputs — where the ball is, where it's heading, where its own paddle is, where the opponent is, and where the ball will land on its side — through 10 hidden units to 3 choices: up / stay / down). Because it can sense the ball's landing spot and is rewarded for being there, it learns to move ahead of the ball and wait, rather than chase it. It is never told the correct move. Each generation, all 28 brains play a full match against the opponent bot; the ones that finished with the best point difference survive and get mutated copies. Selection + mutation, repeated, is enough to discover a winning policy from the score alone. The opponent tracks the ball but moves a little slower than the AI, so it's beatable. Switch the Opponent to Self-play and it plays a copy of itself instead: as it improves, its opponent improves too, so it never stops getting better — the arms-race idea behind AlphaGo. Against the fixed bot you see quick, easy progress that soon plateaus; in self-play it trains against a pool of its past selves (so it can't forget old skills or chase strategies in circles), and each round it's scored against the same fixed bot — a ruler that can't drift — so the curve climbs and then holds, instead of wandering up and down. It all runs in your browser — nothing downloaded, nothing sent anywhere.
