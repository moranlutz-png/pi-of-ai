How it learns: this is reinforcement learning by evolution. A "brain" is a tiny neural net (5 inputs — where the ball is, where it's heading, where the paddle is — through 8 hidden units to 3 choices: up / stay / down). It is never told the correct move. Each generation, all 40 brains play the same ball; the ones that scored best survive and get mutated copies. Selection + mutation, repeated, is enough to discover a good policy from the score alone. It runs entirely in your browser — nothing is downloaded, nothing is sent anywhere.
